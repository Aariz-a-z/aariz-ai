# Planned Maintenance Intervals

This instruction consolidates the running-hour and calendar intervals for machinery maintenance. Where an interval here differs from an equipment manufacturer's manual, the manufacturer's figure applies and this document is corrected.

## Running hour intervals

Turbocharger turbine side water washing is carried out every 250 running hours. Washing is a routine cleaning operation and is not a response to surge, which is covered in the main engine instruction.

The fuel oil purifier is opened and overhauled every 2000 running hours. The bowl is inspected, the seals renewed and the gravity disc confirmed correct for the fuel grade in service.

The lubricating oil purifier is opened and overhauled every 4000 running hours. The interval is longer than that of the fuel oil purifier because the system oil carries a far smaller burden of water and catalytic fines. The two machines are otherwise similar and their intervals are frequently transposed in planning; confirm which machine is being planned before ordering parts.

Main engine piston overhaul, comprising withdrawal, ring renewal, crown inspection and liner measurement, is carried out at 12000 running hours. Scavenge port inspection is carried out far more frequently and is the means by which an early piston or liner problem is found between overhauls.

Fuel injector overhaul and pressure testing is carried out every 4000 running hours, and injectors are changed as a set rather than individually so that all cylinders receive equally atomised fuel.

## Calendar intervals

Boiler safety valves are tested by lifting under steam annually. Easing gear operation is confirmed monthly, which proves the valve is free but says nothing about the set pressure.

Cooling water is tested weekly for nitrite and chloride, and the results are logged. Boiler water is tested for pH and chloride at the same interval but with different reagents and against different limits, both given in their own instructions.

The ultraviolet lamps of the ballast water treatment system are replaced at the interval given by the manufacturer irrespective of apparent condition, for the reason set out in the ballast water instruction.

Lubricating oil samples are landed for laboratory analysis every three months, and additionally whenever water content rises without an identified cause.

Starting air receivers are internally inspected and their relief arrangements confirmed annually. The bursting discs and flame arresters at the cylinder start valves are inspected at the same time.

The emergency generator is run on load monthly, and the automatic start on loss of main power is tested at the same interval. The requirement that it reach the switchboard within a defined time is stated in the emergency instruction.

## Deferral

An interval may be exceeded only with the chief engineer's written approval, recorded with the reason and the revised date. Approval is not given retrospectively to regularise an interval already missed; a missed interval is recorded as missed.

Where a machine is opened early because of a defect, the interval is reset from the date of that work only where the work performed was equivalent in scope to the scheduled overhaul.
